Fleet Insurance for Small Firms

A fleet policy covers every vehicle a business runs under one renewal date and one claims history. Insurers generally require five vehicles before they will quote, though some brokers can place smaller fleets.

Premiums hinge on driver profile and claims record far more than on vehicle value. Firms that also hold commercial property cover can often negotiate a package discount.

When individual policies win

Where vehicles are used seasonally, individual cover sometimes costs less overall. Read our seasonal cover guide before deciding.
