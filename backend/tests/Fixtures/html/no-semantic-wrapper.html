Employers Liability Explained

Employers liability insurance is compulsory for almost every business in the United Kingdom that employs staff, and the certificate must be displayed where employees can read it. The minimum limit set out in legislation is five million pounds, though most insurers issue policies at ten million as standard because the difference in premium is negligible.

Claims usually arise from workplace injury, repetitive strain, or long-tail industrial disease, and the policy responds on the basis of when the exposure happened rather than when the claim was made. That makes continuity of cover important, and it is why brokers keep historical certificates on file for decades.

Firms that use subcontractors should check whether those workers count as employees for the purposes of the Act, because the definition is broader than most business owners expect. Our note on contractor status works through the common cases in detail.
