Commercial Property Insurance Explained

Commercial property insurance protects the buildings and contents a business depends on. Most policies pay out on a reinstatement basis, which means the insurer covers the cost of rebuilding rather than the depreciated value of what was lost.

Setting the sum insured too low triggers the average clause, so it pays to review your business interruption cover at the same time. Brokers usually recommend a formal valuation every three years.

If you operate from more than one site, ask about a portfolio policy. Our guide to fleet insurance covers a similar structure for vehicles, and the regulator's guidance is worth reading too.
